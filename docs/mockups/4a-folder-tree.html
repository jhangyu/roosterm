Roosterm · Mockup 4a · New-session folder picker

Expandable folder tree

A Fluent 2 TreeView rooted at your home folder, opened already expanded down to the folder you used last time, with that row selected and centred. A breadcrumb address bar above it shows — and can take a typed — path.

Trade-offs of the tree direction

  • + Context: every ancestor's siblings stay visible; jumping to the sibling project is one click, no back-navigation.
  • + Desktop-native: arrow keys, type-ahead, Enter to open — the Fluent TreeView keyboard model users know from Explorer / VS Code.
  • − Width on phone: indentation eats a 390 px screen; solved by 12 px indents capped at 4 steps with a pinned-ancestor rebase (screen 03).
  • − Height: the tree wants ~300 px; the hidden-sessions list folds into an accordion on desktop and the picker becomes a second sheet step on phone.

Phone decisions

  • Why two steps: type tiles (3 × 68 px) + hidden sessions already fill ~60 % of a 390 × 844 screen; a tree squeezed under them would show 4 rows. Step 2 shows 12+.
  • Indent 12 px × max 4 = 48 px (+ 28 px chevron + 20 px icon): names always get ≥ 230 px of the 358 px row. The cap is the rebase rule, not a squeeze.
  • Rebase on open: the view's top level is chosen so the selected row sits at indent ≤ 3; above it, the pinned row. Rebase is instant; expanding deeper re-bases with a 200 ms slide.
  • Tap targets: rows 44 px; chevron hit area 44 × 44 at the row start (visual 28 px). Long-press a row = full name + path tooltip.
  • Hidden folders: eye toggle in the sheet header (same state as the desktop switch).
  • Dark terminal behind: the sheet sits on a 40 % scrim over the #1e1e1e terminal; chrome stays light.

Radius on phone (M3)

  • Sheet top corners — extra-large 28
  • Type tiles, Start-in row, hidden list — medium 12
  • Tree rows, buttons, filter — small 8
  • Handle, chips, switch — full
