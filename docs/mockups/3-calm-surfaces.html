Roosterm · Mockup 3 of 3

Calm layered surfaces

Mica base, an Acrylic session rail, and the terminal lifted onto a single focused card. Generous spacing, one accent colour, and elevation instead of borders to say what sits on top of what.

Trade-offs of this direction

  • + Focus & legibility: one elevated terminal card with wide margins reads calmly for long Claude Code sessions; session state lives in the rail as cards, not cramped tabs.
  • − Fewer terminal columns: the 288px rail and 28px card margins cost ~25 columns at 1280px vs. an edge-to-edge tab bar; needs a collapse-rail toggle for wide diffs.
  • − Cost of translucency: Acrylic needs backdrop-filter; on low-end Android WebView it must fall back to the opaque --surface-acrylic colour, and a light terminal diverges from users' dark-terminal habit.

  • Sits on an Acrylic strip directly above the IME; keys are 44 × 44 px minimum (corner-small).

  • Strip scrolls horizontally if more keys are added; arrows stay grouped after a divider.

Sessions on phone

  • The desktop rail collapses into chips under the app bar; the title opens a full session sheet.
  • The terminal card keeps a 10px Mica margin so the layering reads the same as desktop.
